Clearing space fast is easy. Clearing it without regretting something later takes a little order. This guide walks through a safe sequence for tidying Android storage.

## 1. Back up what matters first

Before deleting anything, make sure important photos, videos and documents are backed up, for example to a cloud service or a computer. Check that the backup actually finished. Some backup apps also delete the cloud copy when you delete a photo on the phone, so read your backup app's settings before a big clean-up.

## 2. Find out what is using space

Open **Settings → Storage** to see the biggest categories. DeDup's storage map shows which folders hold the most, so you can focus there. Details are on the [AI storage cleaner page](/ai-storage-cleaner).

## 3. Remove the safest items first

Some things are safe to remove because a copy remains:

- **Exact duplicates.** Keep one copy and remove the rest.
- **Sent copies of chat media** that you also received. The [WhatsApp cleaner](/whatsapp-cleaner) only lists a sent file when an identical received copy exists.
- **APK install files** for apps that are already installed.
- **Empty folders**, which hold nothing.

## 4. Review the judgement calls

These need a look before you delete:

- **Similar photos and burst shots.** Choose the best one in each group. See the [duplicate photo cleaner](/duplicate-photo-cleaner).
- **Near-identical videos.** Play each copy before choosing. See the [duplicate video cleaner](/duplicate-video-cleaner).
- **Large files.** Big videos and documents may be important even if old.
- **Screenshots and receipts.** Some are clutter, some are records you need.

## 5. Delete in a way you can undo

Prefer tools that let you undo. Photos and videos deleted from DeDup's photo and video scanners go to DeDup's Trash first, and can be restored until they expire (30 days by default, adjustable in Settings). Other deletions, such as WhatsApp clean-ups, are permanent, and DeDup asks you to confirm them.

## 6. Organise what you keep

- Move documents you need into one folder, such as `Documents`, rather than leaving them in Downloads.
- Turn off automatic media downloads in chat apps you don't need to keep media from.
- Exclude folders you never want scanned, such as a work folder, in DeDup's settings.

## 7. Make it a habit

A short review every month is easier and safer than one large clean-up when the phone is already full.
